How to read this

Five verdicts, and the difference between two of them is the whole architecture.

Correct

The figure matches a truth computed independently from the loan tape — not from the agent's own output. Since the last bank the oracle also checks that every factual claim in an answer is correct, not only the one the reader asked for, and that a ranked answer's table really holds what was asked.

Correctly declined

The question cannot be answered from this book and the agent said so. Declining is a pass, not a failure. "What changed?" and "What is our largest single-name exposure?" both belong here.

Declined but answerable

The agent refused a question it could have answered. This is the commercial gap — now 12, itemised below. It costs reach. It does not cost trust.

Wrong

An answer was returned and a claim in it is not true. The only verdict that costs trust. Two remain, both named, both pre-existing.

Every recovery is a shared owner

No question-specific rule and no new vocabulary list. Each is one of three shapes: a claim recorded and not carried; a token the guard could not match (Direct- against a list containing direct); or two owners of one governed fact disagreeing.

Stability, not a lucky run

Every recovered question was run six independent times against the live model. All eight are 6/6 correct, and none produced a wrong answer in any run. 215 of 216 calls reached the model.

Why the safety gate does not catch them

Both answers carry every concept the question stated — the right population, the right measure, the right book. What is wrong is the shape of the output, which concept coverage is not built to see and was deliberately not contorted to catch. Both produce a visibly odd artefact — a 24-row list, a 5-point line — rather than a plausible wrong number. That is the failure mode a reviewer catches.
