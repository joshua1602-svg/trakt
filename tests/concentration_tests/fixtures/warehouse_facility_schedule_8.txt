For the purposes of this Schedule 8, “Concentration Limit Denominator” means an amount equal to the greater of: (a) £[33,000,000] and (b) the Current Balance of all the Eligible Mortgage Loans in the Financing Portfolio.
1.	Region
1.	The Current Balance of Eligible Mortgage Loans secured by Mortgaged Properties located in each Geographic Region as a percentage of the Concentration Limit Denominator does not exceed the limits set out below:

Geographic Region	Region Name	Concentration Limit
UKI + UKJ	London and South East	[50]%
UKC	North East	[10]%
UKD	North West	[10]%
UKE	Yorkshire & The Humber	[15]%
UKF	East Midlands	[15]%
UKG	West Midlands	[15]%
UKH	East of England	[25]%
UKK	South West	[20]%
UKM	Scotland	[10]%
UKL	Wales	[10]%

2.	Property Value

1.	The Current Balance of Eligible Mortgage Loans secured by Mortgaged Properties with an Original Valuation greater than £[1,500,000] as a percentage of the Concentration Limit Denominator shall not exceed [10]%. 

2.	The Current Balance of Eligible Mortgage Loans secured by Mortgaged Properties with an Original Valuation less than £[150,000] as a percentage of the Concentration Limit Denominator shall not exceed [10]%.

3.	Principal Balance
1.	The Current Balance of all Eligible Mortgage Loans made to any Borrower with an aggregate initial principal balance greater than £[1,000,000] as a percentage of the Concentration Limit Denominator shall not exceed [10]%. 

2.	The average initial principal balance of Eligible Mortgage Loans in the Financing Portfolio shall not be greater than £[300,000].

4.	Age
1.	The aggregate Current Balance of Eligible Mortgage Loans made to Borrowers where the sole Borrower or the youngest Borrower is below 55 years of age as a percentage of the Concentration Limit Denominator shall not exceed [0]%.
 
5.	Other
1.	The Portfolio Net WAC of Eligible Mortgage Loans shall be greater than or equal to [3.75]% per annum, where “Portfolio Net WAC” means:
a.	the weighted average fixed interest rate of all of the Eligible Mortgage Loans in the Mortgage Portfolio (weighted by Current Balance and expected duration), minus
b.	the Series Fixed Rate from the Final Maturity Date onwards.

2.	The Current Balance of all Eligible Mortgage Loans which have two Borrowers as a percentage of the Concentration Limit Denominator shall not exceed [90]%.
