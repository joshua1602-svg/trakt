SCHEDULE 8

ALDERBRIDGE LENDING PLATFORM - PORTFOLIO CONCENTRATION LIMITS

This Schedule is an invented document created solely for the Trakt product
demonstration. Alderbridge Lending Platform does not exist and this Schedule
does not reproduce, paraphrase or approximate the terms of any real facility,
securitisation or funding agreement.

This Schedule sets out the Concentration Limits applicable to the Portfolio.
All percentages are expressed as a percentage of the Concentration Limit
Denominator (being the aggregate Current Balance of the Portfolio) unless
otherwise stated.

1. GEOGRAPHIC CONCENTRATION

1.1 The aggregate Current Balance of Loans secured on Properties located in
London (Greater London) must not exceed 25% of the Portfolio.

1.2 The aggregate Current Balance of Loans secured on Properties located in
the South East must not exceed 30% of the Portfolio.

1.3 The aggregate Current Balance of Loans secured on Properties located in
any other single region must not exceed 15% of the Portfolio.

1.4 The aggregate Current Balance of Loans secured on Properties located in
Scotland must not exceed 8% of the Portfolio.

2. LOAN SIZE CONCENTRATION

2.1 The Current Balance of any single Loan must not exceed 1% of the Portfolio.

2.2 The aggregate Current Balance of Loans with a Current Balance greater than
GBP 1,000,000 must not exceed 12% of the Portfolio.

3. LOAN TO VALUE

3.1 The weighted average Current Loan to Value of the Portfolio must not exceed
50%.

3.2 No Loan may have a Current Loan to Value greater than 75%.

4. INTEREST RATE

4.1 The aggregate Current Balance of Loans bearing a variable rate of interest
must not exceed 5% of the Portfolio.

5. BORROWER CONCENTRATION

5.1 The aggregate Current Balance attributable to any single Borrower (or group
of connected Borrowers) must not exceed 2% of the Portfolio.

5.2 No single Borrower may have more than 3 Loans in the Portfolio.

6. BORROWER AGE

6.1 The aggregate Current Balance attributable to Borrowers aged over 85 at
origination must not exceed 0.75% of the Portfolio.
